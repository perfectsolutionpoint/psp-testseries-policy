Request account and personal-data deletion

You can request deletion of your PSP Test Series account and associated personal data using either option below. You do not need to reinstall the app to send an email request.

App: PSP Test Series
Android package: com.psp.testseries
Developer: Perfect Solution Point
Privacy contact: perfectsolutionpoint61@gmail.com

Uninstalling the app does not delete your account or data stored on our servers. Deleting your login account removes your ability to sign in with that account; the data-handling details below explain the limits of automated cleanup.

Option 1: Delete from inside the app

  1. Open PSP Test Series and sign in.
  2. Open Profile, then Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.
  4. Wait for the app to report whether deletion completed successfully.

After deletion completes successfully, your login account is removed and you are signed out. If the app reports an error, do not assume deletion completed: contact support using the email option below.

A successful login-account deletion does not confirm removal of every historical record, backup or uploaded image. See the data categories below and contact support about any remaining personal information.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

Email perfectsolutionpoint61@gmail.com with the subject Account Deletion Request. Include the email address registered to your PSP Test Series account and state that you want your account and associated personal data deleted.

Where possible, send the request from your registered email address so we can verify ownership. If you cannot access that inbox, contact the same support address and explain this so we can help with account-ownership verification. Do not send your password, verification codes or payment PINs.

Email an account-deletion request

If the link does not open an email app, copy the support address into your email service and send the request manually.

Our stated service target for handling verified email account-deletion requests is 30 days after ownership verification. This target is not a guarantee that every backup or historical record expires within 30 days. You can use the same email address to ask about request status or remaining data.

What the automated deletion process removes

  • Login account: removed after successful completion, preventing further sign-in with that account.
  • Profile details: name and email are cleared or replaced; phone number, about/bio text, permanent address, PIN code and blood group are cleared from the profile.
  • Device and notification registrations: device registrations, device-change requests and push-notification token registrations are removed. Notifications already queued may still arrive.
  • Profile photos: removal is attempted separately. If photo cleanup fails, an existing public image URL may remain accessible. Contact support to request removal of a remaining image.

These actions concern the live account and related records. They do not establish immediate erasure of backup copies.

Remaining data and retention

The automated account-deletion process does not establish complete removal of the following categories:

  • Additional profile information: gender, nationality and account identifiers may remain in stored records.
  • Learning history: historical test attempts, answers, scores and rankings may remain linked to account identifiers. Replacing a display name does not necessarily make these records anonymous.
  • Earlier transactions: historical purchase or payment records may remain. Payments are disabled in the current app release.
  • Support correspondence: emails or messages sent to support are separate from the app account and are not removed by the in-app deletion action.
  • Backups: copies may remain after the live login account is removed.

Contact perfectsolutionpoint61@gmail.com to request deletion of remaining personal information and ask which records remain, why they are retained and when they will be removed. A fixed retention period for these categories has not been established in this notice; do not assume they are automatically erased after 30 days.

This description of remaining records is not a claim that they are irreversibly anonymised or that every retained record is legally required to be kept.

If an earlier payment involved Razorpay, information it holds independently is also subject to Razorpay's Privacy Policy. Contact our support address for help concerning your earlier purchase records.

Other privacy choices

  • Edit optional profile information in the app or ask support to remove information without closing your account.
  • Request removal of a profile photo through support.
  • Turn off notifications in your device settings; this does not delete your account.
  • Contact support to request access to or correction of your personal information.
