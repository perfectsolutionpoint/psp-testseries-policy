2. Information We Collect

Data We Collect

Account Information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Mobile phone number (required when creating an account)
  • Account identifier and authentication/session information
  • Optional profile details: about/bio, gender, nationality, permanent address and PIN code
  • Optional blood group, entered by you in your profile. This is health-related information stored as a profile detail; the app does not use it to diagnose, treat, or recommend medical care.
  • Profile photo (optional, uploaded by you)
  • Password, submitted to Supabase Auth for authentication; passwords are stored as hashes, not readable profile fields

Device Information

  • An app-generated installation identifier and a fingerprint derived from that identifier and device characteristics, used for device-access checks
  • Device name, model, and operating system version
  • Push notification token when you allow notifications on a supported device

Usage & Performance Data

  • Test attempts, selected answers, scores, accuracy, rank, percentile and time spent on tests/questions
  • Test series access status
  • Login timestamps and session data
  • Support messages you choose to send by email or WhatsApp

Payment Information

Payments are disabled in the current app release. Current test access does not require a purchase, and the current student payment flow does not request payment details.

Users who purchased a test series in an earlier version of the App may still have historical records (UPI reference number, amount, coupon code) stored from that time. If you contact support about an earlier purchase, we may also receive transaction details that you provide. Do not send card credentials, UPI PINs or passwords.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To provide access to test series and track your exam performance
  • To verify your identity and perform device-access checks
  • To send push notifications about new tests, results, and announcements
  • To respond to your support queries

  • To display your optional profile details and your test results and rankings

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Data Sharing

We use the following services to operate the app:

  • Supabase (Database & Authentication) — Privacy Policy
  • Expo / Expo Notifications (Push Notifications) — Privacy Policy

Leaderboards: Your name, score, rank and percentile can be displayed to other signed-in users viewing a test leaderboard. The leaderboard also sends participant account identifiers to their app to associate results with participants. Your phone number, email, address and blood group are not part of the leaderboard display. Do not put sensitive information in your display name.

Profile photos: Uploaded photos are stored in a public Supabase storage bucket. Anyone with the photo URL can access that image. A public image URL is not an access-controlled private album.

Support and external links: If you choose to contact us by email or WhatsApp, your message is processed by those services. Official notice links open external sites with their own privacy practices.

Administrator access: Authorized administrators can access account, profile, device and test information to manage the service, resolve access issues and provide support.

Administrator tools: Where enabled, question text, answer options and uploaded question images are processed by Anthropic to generate explanations, tags or extracted text. These content-preparation features do not use student profiles or test histories as their inputs. Personal information included in an uploaded question or image would also be included in that processing. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

Earlier payments: If you previously used Razorpay to make a purchase, its handling of transaction information is described in Razorpay's Privacy Policy. The current app does not offer that payment flow.

Service infrastructure: Hosting and network providers process connection information, such as your IP address and request metadata, to deliver and secure online requests. Service providers may process information outside your country. The actual processing location depends on the provider and service configuration.

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account information and test data while your account is active to provide your profile, test history and results. Our stated service target for handling verified email account-deletion requests is 30 days. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account or information stored on our servers.

You can delete your account at any time from inside the App (Profile › Settings › Delete Account). The app reports whether the request succeeds. If you cannot access the App, or need follow-up on a remaining profile image or other data, email us at perfectsolutionpoint61@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

Instructions and further information about deletion are on our Account Deletion page.

After a successful in-app deletion response, the login account is removed. The deletion process clears or replaces name, email, phone, about text, address, PIN code and blood group and removes device registrations, device-change requests and push-token registrations. Profile-image removal is attempted separately and can require support follow-up.

Historical test and transaction records, account identifiers, and some additional profile fields may remain after the automated process. Backup copies may also remain after the live account is removed. We do not represent this process as immediate deletion or irreversible anonymisation of every associated record. Contact our privacy support address to request follow-up removal of remaining personal information and details of any retained records. A fixed automatic expiry for historical records and backups is not specified in this policy.

6. Permissions and optional features

  • Photos: The profile-photo feature opens your device's image picker so you can choose an image to upload. Image access depends on the permissions and picker available on your operating system. Uploading a photo is optional.
  • Camera: The Android configuration includes camera permission, but the current profile-photo screen uses the image library. Test-taking does not require camera access.
  • Notifications: With your device's permission, we register a push token to deliver app announcements and test-related notifications. You can turn notifications off in system settings.
  • Administrator imports: Administrative content tools can process files or question images selected by an administrator.

The current student app does not use GPS location, your contact list or microphone for its learning features. The app does not display advertising or use your profile information for personalized advertising.

Blood group is optional health-related information used only as a profile detail, not for diagnosis, treatment, medical advice or Health Connect. You do not need to provide it to take tests.

7. Security

The app uses the following measures to protect your data:

  • Supabase Auth handles password authentication; passwords are not stored as readable profile fields
  • The production app is configured to use HTTPS for its authentication and backend requests
  • Device identifiers support device-access checks
  • Authentication and database access policies control access; administrator access, shared leaderboard information and public profile-image URLs are described above

The app stores sign-in/session information and settings locally so you can use the service. The native app also keeps diagnostic logs on your device; its local logger does not automatically upload those logs to a crash-reporting service. Logs or screenshots you choose to send to support are handled as support information.

No method of electronic transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data.

8. Children's Privacy

PSP Test Series is intended for students and competitive-exam aspirants aged 13 and above, including students aged 13–17 and adults.

If you are below the age at which you can independently consent to data processing in your country, involve your parent or guardian as required by applicable law. Parents and guardians can contact our privacy support address about a student's information. An age range in this policy does not itself verify a user's age or record parental consent.

Our App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.
